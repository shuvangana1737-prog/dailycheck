DAILY CHECK - offline app for iPhone, no Mac needed
(You upload these files to a free website once, then add it to your Home Screen.)

STEP 1 - Free GitHub account: https://github.com (sign up)
STEP 2 - Make a repository: "+" > New repository > name it dailycheck > Public > Create
STEP 3 - Upload: "uploading an existing file" link > choose ALL files from this folder
         (index.html, sw.js, manifest.json, xlsx.full.min.js and the icon-*.png files) > Commit changes
STEP 4 - Settings > Pages > Source: "Deploy from a branch" > Branch: main, folder /(root) > Save
STEP 5 - Wait 1-2 minutes. Your address will be:
         https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/dailycheck/
STEP 6 - On the iPhone, open that address in Safari (the blue compass browser).
         Wait for it to fully load once while online.
         Tap Share > Add to Home Screen > Add.
STEP 7 - Open it from the Home Screen icon. It now works with no internet.

Notes
- Your goals/data stay on your phone only; the website holds just the app, not your data.
- Import your .goals file inside the app (menu > Import), then export a backup now and then.
- To use another icon, replace the icon-*.png files before uploading.
